# How to Use the Geospatial Browser

</div>

The Geospatial Browser allows you to search and find VPS-Activated locations across the globe. You can select the locations you want to use for your project and download the associated 3D mesh assets.

    1.  Go to <a href="https://lightship.dev/signin" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://lightship.dev/signin</a>.

    2.  Select a location. This will show you the associated mesh.
3.  Download the Mesh.
    1.  Once you’ve selected a location, click **Download Mesh**. <img src="https://www.nianticspatial.com/docs/assets/images/gsb_download_mesh-97c95619542f8abd61415248b8d112c8.png" style="width:100.0%" alt="Default Anchor" />

## Using Filters<a href="#using-filters" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Using Filters" title="Direct link to Using Filters">​</a>

The filter (upper left button on the Geospatial Browser map view) allows you to search through various types of locations.

<img src="https://www.nianticspatial.com/docs/assets/images/gsb_categories-9b59ae1e269e9ac66894d3815afc2bad.png" style="width:50.0%" alt="Category Filter" />

You can choose to filter on:

### Playable Area<a href="#playable-area" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Playable Area" title="Direct link to Playable Area">​</a>

The sliders let you set the minimum and maximum area size to show (the graph illustrates what proportions of the current location are selected).

### Category<a href="#category" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Category" title="Direct link to Category">​</a>

- **Objects**: Objects include benches, water fountains, signs, trail markers, etc.
- **Sports/Rec**: Indoor and outdoor locations such as sports fields, stadiums, bowling alleys, ice or roller rinks, etc.
- **Art**: Indoor and outdoor pieces of art, from photographs to statues and murals.
- **Buildings**: Buildings including offices, restaurants, retail establishments, government buildings, etc.
- **Outdoors**: Outdoor locations such as gardens, urban plazas, playgrounds, hiking trails, bodies of water, etc.
- **Transportation**: Train stations, bus terminals, footbridges, etc.
- **Other**: Other types of VPS Locations.

### Location Type<a href="#location-type" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Location Type" title="Direct link to Location Type">​</a>

- **Public**: Locations that are available in all published projects. This excludes private locations (those that are only accessible to your workspace)
- **Test**: Locations of test scans (see [How to Manage Test Scans](https://www.nianticspatial.com/docs/nsdk/how-to/vps/tooling/manage_test_scans/) for more information)

### Status<a href="#status" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Status" title="Direct link to Status">​</a>

Locations in the real world get nominated to be on the Niantic Map. Users can provide scans for approved locations and with enough good scans, locations become VPS-activated and are assigned a quality rating. The quality of a location refers to a user's ability to localize at any time of the day at that particular location.

- **Good**: Show Production Quality locations. These are locations with several scans in all types of lighting. Use locations with Production Quality to ensure the best experience when you test your app in the wild.
- **Fair**: Show Experimental Quality locations. These are locations with only the minimum number of scans or a majority of scans in one type of lighting.
- **Not Activated**: Approved locations that do not have enough submitted scans will be labeled **Not Activated**. To request activation, a minimum of ten viable scans must be submitted and two scans need to be at least five hours apart. Click on "View Details" to see the Scan Progress and Activation Status. See [How to Create a Public Location](https://www.nianticspatial.com/docs/nsdk/how-to/vps/tooling/create_vps_activated_location/) for more information.

## Location Tags<a href="#location-tags" class="hash-link" aria-label="Direct link to Location Tags" title="Direct link to Location Tags">​</a>

In the Geospatial Browser location detail view, you will see two different types of location tags:

- **Public**: Public locations have been approved by Niantic’s Trust & Safety team and have met the required criteria of safety and public accessibility. These locations may be used in published projects.
- **Private**: Private locations are only accessible to your Workspace. They can be created by scanning a location using Niantic’s Scaniverse app. Private locations are intended for use during development and, due to possible performance issues, should not be included in a published project.
