# enum Localizability

</div>

(Niantic.Lightship.AR.VpsCoverage.CoverageArea.Localizability)

The localizability quality is split into CoverageAreas above a quality threshold marked as PRODUCTION, and CoverageAreas that have a lower localizability quality marked as EXPERIMENTAL.
